Google Antigravity 256K Context Limit & Headless Policy Change Archive

Google Antigravity advertises and displays in its client interface a 1,048,576 (1M) token context window for Gemini 4 Argon and Gemini 3.8 Flash. However, static disassembly of the official client binaries and local SQLite session databases show that a hardcoded machine-code instruction clamps the automatic context compaction threshold at 256,000 tokens (25% of the advertised window). Around the same period, Google support staff reversed 10 prior written forum confirmations regarding local subprocess invocations of agy -p from third-party tools by updating a non-contractual FAQ page without amending the formal Additional Terms of Service (ToS).
